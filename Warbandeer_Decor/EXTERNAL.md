# Warbandeer: Decor

**An account-wide housing decor collection tracker.** Housing decor is shared across your whole account, so this is one filterable catalog of every decor the game knows about — showing at a glance what you own, what you're still missing, and what you're chasing.

Open it with `/housingdecor`, or find it as a **Housing Decor** tab inside the main **Warbandeer** addon.

## Highlights

- **One row per decor** — quality-bordered icon, owned count (storage + placed), source text on hover, and a gold star on decor that still grants a one-time **House XP** bonus you haven't claimed.
- **Filter & search** — by category and subcategory, unowned-only, wanted-only, or by name.
- **Wanted list** — Shift-click to flag decor (saved account-wide); `/housingdecor wanted` opens a copyable shopping list. Pair with **ShadowsOfUI-HousingVendor** to see your wanted stars right on the icons at decor vendors.

The catalog is read live from the game's housing system every session, so it can never fall out of sync.

## Dependencies

- **LibNAddOn**, **LibNUI** — install automatically. (No Warbandeer_Characters needed — decor is account-wide.)
