# Shadows of UI: Recipe Learners

**Adds profession recipe-knowledge lines to tooltips**, telling you at a glance which of your characters can learn — or already know — a recipe, and which of your **guildmates** can craft it.

Hover any recipe, pattern, schematic, formula or design — in your bags, at a vendor, in the Auction House, or a chat link — and the tooltip gains:

- **Learnable by:** the alts that have the profession but haven't learned it yet (too-low-skill characters shown in red),
- **Known by:** the characters that already know it (main crafter first),
- **Guild crafters:** on the Crafting Order browse list, the guildmates who can craft it (online first).

Lists are ordered main-crafter-first (via Warbandeer) and capped so the tooltip stays small.

## Dependencies

- **LibNAddOn**, **Warbandeer_Characters** (per-character profession + recipe data) — install automatically.
- Optional: **Warbandeer** (main/secondary crafter ordering).
