# Shadows of UI: Profession Commissions

**Shows what a crafting order actually rewards — right in the order list, without hovering.**

Patron (NPC) crafting orders hand out bonus items or currency on top of the gold commission, but Blizzard hides them behind a single generic treasure-chest icon. This addon **removes that chest and draws the actual reward icons** in its place, next to the commission amount — each with its full tooltip on hover and a quality-coloured border.

- **Rare rewards glow gold** — an Epic-or-better reward (item or currency) is lit so the one order worth stopping for doesn't slide past while you scroll.
- **An "Info" column** replaces the stock Reagents text with two at-a-glance icons: a **first-craft bonus** flag (still earnable / recipe not learned) and a **who-supplies-the-reagents** status (customer provides all / you provide some or all). It still sorts by reagent state.

## Dependencies

- **LibNAddOn** — installs automatically.
