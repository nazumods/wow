# Warbandeer

**Your whole warband at a glance.** Warbandeer is a multi-view dashboard for every character you play — gear, professions, gold, reputations, titles, playtime, crafting plans, collections and more — collected automatically as you log between characters and presented in one clean window.

Open it with `/warband` or `/wb`, the minimap button, or the addon-compartment menu, and switch views with the icon rail on the left.

## Highlights

- **Overview & Summary** — warband-wide wealth, top alts, tier-set completion, faction progress, the Traveler's Log, and (once you own a house) per-house level, favor and endeavor progress. A one-row-per-character summary with a faction control that merges your Alliance and Horde rosters into a single list.
- **Great Vault** — every max-level character's vault at a glance, with the exact reward item level per slot and this week's raid lockouts.
- **Detail** — a deep dive on any character: gear, secondary-stat analysis, suggested upgrades you can equip right now, recommended consumables (with ClassCodex), cosmetic glyphs, class unlocks and class mounts, plus Hunter pet stables and Warlock demon rosters.
- **Gear, Roles, Races, Professions, Crafting, Playtime, Reputations, Titles** — coverage of your whole roster, each reachable directly (`/wb gear`, `/wb profs`, …).
- **Bars, Collected & Housing Decor** — an action-bar profile browser and transmog/decor collection grids, when the optional sibling addons are installed.

**Keybinding:** bind *Toggle Warbandeer window* under Esc → Options → Key Bindings. Tap to open; hold for a radial view selector under your cursor.

## Dependencies

- **LibNAddOn**, **LibNUI**, and **Warbandeer_Characters** (the data layer that does the collecting) — all install automatically.
- Optional: **Warbandeer_Bars** (Bars view), **Warbandeer_Collected** (Collected view), **Warbandeer_Decor** (Housing Decor view), **ShadowsOfUI-Upgrade** (gear-upgrade markers), and **ClassCodex** (stat targets + consumables).
