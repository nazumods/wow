# Shadows of UI: Artisan

**Adds a currency badge to the profession window** showing how much of that profession's expansion crafting currency (the "Artisan's … Moxie" family) the current character holds — right beside Blizzard's Concentration readout, so you see your Moxie without digging into the currency tracker.

- Follows whichever profession you have open, and also shows on the **Crafting Orders** tab and on the **spellbook's Professions page** (every profession's Moxie at a glance).
- **Hover the badge** for an account-wide breakdown: every character with that profession, class-coloured, with how much each holds — main crafter first.

## Dependencies

- **LibNAddOn**, **Warbandeer_Characters** (records each character's professions and currency amounts) — install automatically.
- Optional: **Warbandeer** (main/secondary crafter ordering).
