# Shadows of UI: Quest XP

**Shows what percentage of a level a quest's XP reward is worth**, right next to the XP reward in the quest log.

Open your quest log (the world map) and select a quest that awards XP: next to the XP number in the "You will receive" section, you'll see a muted `(3%)` — how much of your current level that reward is worth. The percentage is relative to your current level's XP requirement, so it stays a stable measure of a quest's worth no matter how much you've already banked.

No settings, no saved data — install it and quest.

## Dependencies

- **LibNAddOn** — installs automatically.
