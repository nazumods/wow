# Warbandeer: Collected

**A transmog collection tracker for your whole warband.** A grid of instance tier sets (and every weapon appearance) versus your characters, showing how many appearances each character can still collect — so you always know what's left and who to run it on next.

Open it with `/collected`.

## Highlights

- **Armor & Weapons grids** — tier sets or weapon looks, sortable and filterable by expansion and source, with red→green "how close am I" shading and per-set piece breakdowns on hover.
- **3D dressing room** — click any set to preview it on any playable race, cycle each slot through worn / hidden / no-transmog, and arrow between classes and difficulty tiers without reopening.
- **Wanted & tier ranking** — Shift-click to flag what you're chasing (gold star + running count) and rate any look S / A / B / C / F, saved account-wide.
- **Account-wide outfit library** — build, save, and share looks that follow you to every alt (the game's own saved sets are per-character). Export/import via the game's `/customset` string or a clickable chat link, or read a look straight off a player you target.
- **PTR preview** — peek at the transmog sets and weapons coming in the next patch, and flag them wanted before they ship.

## Dependencies

- **LibNAddOn**, **LibNUI**, **LibNUI-ModelViewer** (the 3D viewer), and **Warbandeer_Characters** (character + lockout data) — all install automatically.
