# Shadows of UI: Upgrade

**Finds gear upgrades for your whole warband — and flags gear that's missing (or carrying the wrong) enchant, or has an empty gem socket —** and shows them right where you make keep-or-sell decisions: on item tooltips, and across Warbandeer's Summary, Gear and Detail views.

The point is to stop hoarding. Instead of holding ten "just in case" one-handers, you can see at a glance which characters an item actually upgrades — and which copy is best — so everything else can be vendored.

- **Smart upgrade detection** — respects armour type, weapon proficiency, primary stat, and two-handers-vs-dual-wield; tags each candidate **good stats** or **off-stats** against the spec's priority. Fully standalone (built-in stat weightings), no other addon required.
- **Missing / wrong enchants & empty sockets** — flagged warband-wide (read straight from the item), each with the recommended enchant/gem to apply. With **ClassCodex** installed, the picks are its per-spec Wowhead recommendations; without it, a solid built-in fallback is used.
- **Held vs. better elsewhere** — an upgrade in a character's own bags is marked green; a better copy in the warband bank is flagged gold with a "move it over" note.

## Dependencies

- **LibNAddOn**, **Warbandeer_Characters** (equipped/bag/bank gear data) — install automatically.
- Optional: **Warbandeer** (Summary/Gear/Detail markers) and **ClassCodex** (per-spec enchant/gem/consumable picks).
