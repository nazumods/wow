# Shadows of UI: Warband Inventory

**Adds a "Warband Inventory" block to item tooltips**, telling you at a glance how many of an item your whole account is holding — and where.

Hover any item — in your bags, at a vendor, in the Auction House, or a chat link — and the tooltip gains a short breakdown:

- one **class-coloured line per character** that has any, with a `(Bags, Bank, Mail)` source breakdown (cross-realm characters get their realm appended),
- the shared **warband (account) bank**,
- each **guild bank** you can see,
- and a **Total owned** grand total.

**Hold Shift to hide the block.** `/swinv <itemID or link>` prints the same breakdown to chat.

## Dependencies

- **LibNAddOn**, **Warbandeer_Characters** (provides the per-character bag/bank counts) — install automatically.
