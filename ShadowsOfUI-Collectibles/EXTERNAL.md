# Shadows of UI: Collectible Tints

**Tints the icons of items you already know — and items you can still collect —** on the merchant window and the Auction House browse list. See at a glance whether a recipe, toy, mount, pet or housing decoration is worth buying, without hovering every single item.

- **Already known** → tinted your chosen colour (blue by default). Covers profession recipes, toys, mounts, battle pets (including caged AH pets), and housing decor.
- **Still collectible** → tinted green (something you don't have yet).
- **Recipes are checked across your whole warband** — a pattern any of your characters already knows shows as known even while you're on someone else (via **Warbandeer_Characters**).
- A live-preview settings panel with seven bold tint presets, a custom colour picker, and optional desaturation.

## Dependencies

- **LibNAddOn** — installs automatically.
- Optional: **Warbandeer_Characters** (cross-alt recipe check) and **ShadowsOfUI-HousingVendor** (shared decor owned-state).
